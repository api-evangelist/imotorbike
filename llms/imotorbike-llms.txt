## Selling A Motorbike (Malaysia)

- Sell page: https://imotorbike.com/my/en/sell-motorbike/
- Process: send your details, book a free inspection, get a free quotation, then get paid fast.
- Documents to prepare: motorcycle grant certificate; identification card (IC) or passport; maintenance record, original parts and spare keys (if any).
- Active loan: iMotorbike can handle the loan settlement on your behalf; if the motorcycle's value has a surplus over the loan it is remitted to you, and if the loan balance exceeds the value you cover the remaining fee.
- Ownership transfer: iMotorbike assists with the ownership exchange at the branch (Malaysian sellers do not need to visit the JPJ office); this process usually takes 1 day.
- Payout: iMotorbike needs the bank account details of the registered owner to initiate the transaction.
